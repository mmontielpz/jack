# Future visual identity brief

JACK has no dedicated logo, icon or social-preview assets in this checkout.
These are future design requirements, not completed branding or a release gate
for the CLI. Keep the product name JACK.

A future design should provide:

- An original logo/mark and a small app/CLI icon that remains recognizable at
  small sizes, including monochrome use.
- A GitHub social preview/banner with readable text, safe cropping and accessible
  contrast across light and dark contexts; no invented capabilities or metrics.
- Editable SVG/vector source, with documented exports and an accessible textual
  description. Check small-size legibility rather than relying on color alone.
- Clear creator ownership, permission and asset licensing compatible with the
  project. Record attribution and provenance before committing assets.

Do not reuse external trademarks or third-party logos. Do not add placeholder
assets or imply an endorsement by Tailscale, Apple, or any other vendor.
